## 5. Brand, tone & legal constraints (recap)

- **Name:** always `novetest`, lowercase (core CLI). Family brand = **Novetest**; family products =
  **Novetest Console**, **Novetest Team**.
- **License:** novetest is **Apache-2.0**. Show "free & open source (Apache-2.0)" prominently and
  link the license. **No pricing or free claims** for Novetest Console / Novetest Team — present them
  as **Planned** only.
- **Brand feel:** homage to iLovePDF's clarity, but more modern and geeky / developer-flavored.
- **Voice:** developer-credible, precise, anti-hype. Never imply novetest is itself an AI/LLM.
- **Honesty rule:** every capability claim carries its Live/Building/Planned badge.
- **Privacy consistency:** match the product's "nothing leaves your machine" stance — minimal /
  cookieless tracking or none.
- **Platforms:** Linux & macOS today; Windows is Planned.
