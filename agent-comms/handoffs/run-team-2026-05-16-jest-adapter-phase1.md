## Chosen normalizer option + rationale

**Option (a) — generalized internal dispatch.** Public `normalize_native_result` retained as the single engine-agnostic surface; the function now dispatches on `native_engine_context.engine_name` to a per-engine private `_normalize_<engine>_payload`. The pytest logic was extracted verbatim into `_normalize_pytest_payload` so the existing behavior is byte-equivalent under the new shape (verified by re-running the existing 11 normalizer tests — all green).

**Rationale:** at two adapters the refactor is ~30 LOC of restructuring vs Option (b)'s ~10 LOC of a parallel public function. Option (a) keeps a single surface for `engine.py` to call (no engine-aware dispatch needed at the engine layer), and the dispatcher is structurally extensible to a 3rd adapter without further refactoring. Per Karpathy "wait for the third instance" the dispatcher is the *minimum* abstraction the second adapter motivates — a full registry pattern (`adapters/registry.py` keyed by name) is deferred until adapter #3 lands.
